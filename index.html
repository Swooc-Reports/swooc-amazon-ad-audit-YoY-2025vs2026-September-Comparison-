Executive Audit Summary: Key Findings

Overall portfolio revenue and ad spend both decreased while aggregate campaign ACOS remained flat at 30.51%. The audit reveals 41 out of 61 SKUs underwent intentional advertising spend cuts totaling -$58.06K. While campaign efficiency was preserved on paper, reduced spend led to top-of-funnel impression contraction and overall sales losses of -$167.53K across those SKUs, partially offset by +$37.14K in new product launches.
